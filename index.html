Questions

Fair things to ask.

Does one subscription really cover us both?

Yes. When one of you gets Plus, you both have it — on both phones. Charging a couple twice for the same relationship felt wrong, so we don't.

One of us has an iPhone, the other Android. Does it work?

Yes. Dote ships on iOS and Android, and mixed couples are first-class — widgets, moments and moods work the same in both directions.

What happens to our stuff if we break up?

You can export your memories first, always. Unpairing starts a 30-day window in case you change your minds — either of you can invite the other to rejoin, and everything comes back. After 30 days it's permanently deleted, photos included. That's a promise we keep, not a threat we sell.

When can we get it?

Dote is in on-device testing now and launching on the App Store and Google Play soon. Anything else on your mind — support@dotelove.com. A human reads it.
