Delete your Dote account & data

Applies to the Dote app by O9Tech (com.o9tech.dote) · takes effect immediately, permanent after 30 days

Delete your account in the app

  • Open Dote → Us tab → Privacy & data → Delete account.
  • You'll be offered a full export of your memories first — it's always free.
  • Confirm, and your account is deleted. No email, call, or approval needed.

Or request deletion by email

Can't open the app (lost phone, uninstalled)? Email support@dotelove.com from any address, tell us the display name you used, and we'll delete the account for you. A human reads it.

What gets deleted, and when

  • Deleted: your account (the anonymous ID and any linked sign-in), your display name and timezone, your device push token, and every moment you own — photos, notes, drawings, moods, reactions, taps and countdowns, including the files in storage.
  • The shared couple space follows the unpair rules: it's archived for a 30-day change-of-heart window (either partner can restore it by rejoining), then permanently deleted, photos included.
  • Kept: nothing after that window. Delivery telemetry (timing numbers, never content) and any safety reports are deleted along with the couple they belong to.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Any single moment can be deleted in the app (open the moment → ⋯ → Delete). It disappears from both partners' apps immediately and is permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.
  • Unpairing (Us tab → Unpair) removes the shared space on the same 30-day schedule while your account itself remains.
  • For anything else — export, correction, or a specific erasure request — email support@dotelove.com.

The full detail lives in our privacy policy.
